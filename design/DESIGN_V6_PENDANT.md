# Pendant case v6 — design (user image, 2026-11-18 16:52)
Reference: design/pendant_case_concept.jpg (FRONT / RIGHT SIDE / REAR)

- Ivory matte BUMPER case wrapping the watch's edge; front glass and back
  sensor fully open (rear view shows sensor through a round window).
- The watch lives in the case CROWN-UP permanently: crown at top, exposed,
  flanked by two guard humps of the bumper (crown is USED — must turn/press).
- Charging contacts end up at the BOTTOM, exposed by an open bottom segment
  of the bumper (front view: two contact dots visible on the rim at bottom).
- Cord attaches at LEFT and RIGHT eyelets at mid height (integrated lugs
  with vertical oval holes); no top tower, no bail ring.
- Because worn orientation == charging orientation (crown up, contacts
  down), docking is: lift off neck, set straight down into the dock.
  The v5 dock's seat/puck geometry (docked pose) already matches this
  orientation; guides/cheeks may need rework for the bumper outline
  (no tower on the left; eyelets protrude at both sides at mid height).
